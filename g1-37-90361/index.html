Bước 3 · Luyện nói

Tự trình bày theo mạch ý

Quay hai video: Video 1 thuyết trình theo mindmap; Video 2 trả lời năm câu hỏi bằng câu tiếng Anh đầy đủ.

  1. A force: A force can change motion; Gravity is one force
  2. Gravity pulls: Earth pulls objects toward its centre; A dropped pen falls
  3. Throw upward: A ball rises after a throw; Gravity helps bring it down
  4. Compare: The Moon also has gravity; Its pull is weaker than Earth’s

Câu hỏi nghe hiểu

  1. What force pulls a dropped pen toward Earth?
  2. Why does a ball come back down after you throw it up?
  3. Does gravity stop working at night?
  4. Is there gravity on the Moon?
  5. How is the Moon’s pull different from Earth’s?
